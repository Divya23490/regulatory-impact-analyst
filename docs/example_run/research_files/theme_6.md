# Theme 6: Cyber Threat Information Sharing

## Regulatory Requirements
Financial entities may exchange cyber threat information and intelligence within trusted communities, provided that such exchanges comply fully with personal data protection rules (GDPR).

## Affected Policies
- **POL-012 (Threat Intelligence & Information Sharing)**: Governs the consumption of commercial threat intelligence feeds, but lacks a framework or governance rules for actively sharing cyber threat information and indicators of compromise within trusted financial sector communities in compliance with GDPR.

## Gap Analysis
1. **Trusted Community Sharing Framework**: POL-012 must be updated to establish protocols for participating in information-sharing arrangements with trusted financial industry communities.
2. **GDPR Compliance Safeguards**: Clear legal and privacy guidelines must be integrated into POL-012 to ensure that any threat intelligence sharing involving personal data strictly adheres to GDPR requirements.
