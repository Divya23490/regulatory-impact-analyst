# Theme 1: ICT Risk Management Framework

## Regulatory Requirements
- Financial entities must establish a sound, comprehensive, and well-documented ICT risk management framework as part of their overall risk management system to address ICT risk quickly, efficiently, and comprehensively [dora::art6::p1].
- The framework must include strategies, policies, procedures, ICT protocols, and tools necessary to protect all information assets, ICT assets, and physical components/infrastructure (premises, data centres, sensitive areas) from damage, unauthorized access, or usage [dora::art6::p2].
- The framework requires a digital operational resilience strategy setting out risk tolerance levels, impact tolerance for disruptions, information security objectives, ICT reference architecture, incident detection mechanisms, testing implementation, and communication strategies [dora::art6::p8].
- Financial entities (other than microenterprises) must assign the responsibility for managing and overseeing ICT risk to a control function with appropriate independence, segregation, and audit functions following the three lines of defence model [dora::art6::p4].

## Affected Internal Policies
- [policy::POL-001] — ICT Risk Management Framework: Defines identification, assessment, and treatment of ICT risks, but lacks formalised annual board review and explicit digital operational resilience strategy elements.
- [policy::POL-011] — Board Training & Competence Policy: Lacks dedicated ICT/operational-resilience modules for board and management oversight.

## Gap Analysis
- **Governance & Strategy**: POL-001 does not fully formalize the digital operational resilience strategy or risk tolerances required by DORA Art. 6(8). Annual board review is not formally established.
- **Three Lines of Defence / Independence**: While risk functions exist, formal segregation of ICT risk control functions needs audit and enhancement to fully align with DORA Art. 6(4).
- **Board Competence**: POL-011 lacks specific training curricula for the management body regarding digital operational resilience.
