# Theme 1: ICT Risk Management Framework

## Regulatory Requirements
DORA requires financial entities to maintain a documented ICT risk management framework that is approved and reviewed at least annually by the management body. The management body bears final responsibility for managing ICT risk and must complete regular ICT risk training.

## Affected Policies
- **POL-001 (ICT Risk Management Framework)**: Currently defines identification/assessment/treatment of ICT risks, but annual board review is not yet formalised.
- **POL-011 (Board Training & Competence Policy)**: Covers induction and annual training plans, but lacks a mandatory ICT and operational resilience training module for board members.

## Gap Analysis
1. **Board Review & Accountability**: POL-001 must be updated to explicitly mandate formal annual approval and review of the ICT risk management framework by the management body.
2. **Board Training**: POL-011 must be revised to incorporate regular, structured ICT risk training specifically tailored for the management body to satisfy DORA's ultimate responsibility mandate.
