# Theme 2: Incident Management and Reporting

## Regulatory Requirements
Entities must classify ICT-related incidents by severity using common criteria (clients affected, data losses, duration, geographical spread, economic impact). Major incidents must be reported to the competent authority via an initial notification, an intermediate report, and a final report within regulatory deadlines.

## Affected Policies
- **POL-003 (Incident Management Standard)**: Establishes an internal incident severity scale (P1-P4) and operational triage, but lacks regulatory notification workflows, common severity classification criteria aligned with DORA, and statutory reporting deadlines for initial, intermediate, and final reports.

## Gap Analysis
1. **Severity Classification**: POL-003 must be updated to adopt DORA's common criteria (clients affected, data losses, duration, geographical spread, and economic impact) rather than relying solely on internal operational severity.
2. **Regulatory Reporting Workflow**: A formal incident reporting procedure must be integrated into POL-003 to govern the tripartite notification process (initial, intermediate, and final reports) to the competent authority within prescribed timelines.
