# Theme 3: Digital Operational Resilience Testing

## Regulatory Requirements
Financial entities must maintain a digital operational resilience testing programme including vulnerability assessments and scans. Significant entities must conduct threat-led penetration testing (TLPT) at least every three years, executed by qualified testers.

## Affected Policies
- **POL-004 (Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery)**: Covers annual DR testing and RTO/RPO per service, but its scenario library is currently limited to data-centre loss and does not encompass comprehensive operational resilience testing scenarios.
- **POL-007 (Penetration Testing Standard)**: Mandates annual external penetration testing of internet-facing systems, but contains no provision for threat-led penetration testing (TLPT) every three years or qualification criteria for TLPT testers.

## Gap Analysis
1. **TLPT Programme**: POL-007 must be expanded to establish a formal threat-led penetration testing (TLPT) framework conducted at least every three years by qualified internal or external testers for significant systems/entities.
2. **Testing Scope**: POL-004 and POL-007 should be aligned to ensure vulnerability assessments, advanced testing, and broader scenario-based resilience tests are integrated into a cohesive testing programme.
