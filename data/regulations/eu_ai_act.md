# EU AI Act — synthetic working excerpt

> Paraphrased, shortened study excerpt for this demo. Not the legal text.
> See Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 for anything real.

## Risk tiers
AI systems are grouped into: prohibited practices, high-risk systems, limited-risk
systems (transparency duties), and minimal-risk systems.

## Banking-relevant high-risk use
AI used for creditworthiness assessment and credit scoring of natural persons is
high-risk, as is AI used for risk assessment and pricing in life and health
insurance. Fraud-detection systems are carved out from high-risk in some cases.

## Obligations for high-risk systems
Providers and deployers must ensure: a risk management system across the
lifecycle, data governance for training/validation/test sets, technical
documentation, automatic logging, human oversight, and appropriate accuracy,
robustness and cybersecurity. Deployers that are banks must also run a
fundamental rights impact assessment before putting a high-risk system into use.

## Transparency
People must be told when they interact with an AI system, and AI-generated
content must be marked as such.

## Governance
Each entity should designate accountable owners for AI systems, keep an inventory
of AI systems in use, and ensure staff operating them have adequate AI literacy.

## Timeline
Prohibitions apply first, followed by general-purpose AI rules, followed by the
bulk of high-risk obligations on a later date set in the Regulation.
